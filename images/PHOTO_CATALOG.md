## 🔄 Photo Organization Best Practices

1. **Logos & Brand:** Keep in `logos/` — referenced directly in nav
2. **Team Photos:** Use processed variants for consistency across the site
3. **Property Photos:** 
   - Small thumbnails for portfolio grid
   - High-res for project detail pages
   - Before/after pairs for case studies
4. **File Naming:** Use descriptive, lowercase names with hyphens (e.g., `groton-interior.jpg`)
5. **Formats:**
   - PNG: Logos, transparent backgrounds
   - JPG: Photos, high-quality interiors/exteriors
   - WebP: Modern format for additional compression (fallback to JPG)
